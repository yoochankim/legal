Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

The tools listed on this site are used only by their author on his own computer. No one else is meant to sign in.

When the author signs in with Google, the tool receives an access token for his own account. It uses that token to read and manage his own Gmail, Calendar and Drive data. Tokens and data stay on his computer. Nothing is sent to any other server, sold, or shared.

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
